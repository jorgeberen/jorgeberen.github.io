01 about

I'm a full-stack engineer with a path that didn't start in software: I was an acoustic engineer, then a secondary-school Spanish teacher in the UK, before settling into code. That mix shows up in how I work — I like explaining things clearly, listening to what people actually need, and turning that into something that works well, delights the users and solves real problems.

Since 2021 I've worked remotely on product teams, from a no-code platform to a production crypto-payments ecosystem: React and Next.js on the front, NestJS and Django behind them, and React Native when it needs to live on a phone. These days I also work heavily with AI-assisted and agentic development to move faster without lowering the bar.
